# Develop the story

Read project settings, guidelines, bible and the current packet. Use
[the premise worksheet](../../templates/premise-alternatives.md) for alternatives
and [the craft guide](../../docs/guidelines.md) for the chosen audience.

When alternatives are requested, make their obstacles, attempts or decisive
choices differ; changing the setting alone is insufficient. Compare emotional
stakes, hero agency, causal strength and visual possibilities. Follow an existing
selection, or choose within the user's delegated scope and state the reason.

Write the purpose separately from the manuscript: intent, teaching goal if any,
lesson, theme and the beats that convey them. Build the beat sheet before final
dialogue. Each failed attempt should change what the hero knows or does. Check
that the resolution follows from the hero's action and an established possibility.

Draft to the selected word budget without losing motives, feelings or consequences.
Keep stable beat IDs, cast and location references; distinguish reading order
from chronology. Record proposed facts and open threads in the packet. Validate
and build the workspace, then report the selected premise, changed story and
remaining editorial questions. Route composition or review work only as needed.
