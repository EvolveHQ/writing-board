# Compose the book

Read the packet, project page allowance and guidelines, then
[composition guidance](../../docs/composition.md). Allocate non-story pages before
assigning story spreads. Do not treat every beat as a mandatory separate spread.

For each spread identify the dominant action, emotional change, page-turn reveal,
word contribution, art contribution, framing and text zone. Use
[the scene brief](../../templates/scene-art-brief.md) for production detail.
Preserve causes and choices when shortening text; reduce redundant description
first. Vary scale and viewpoint for meaning, not to satisfy a mechanical quota.

Reserve safe text areas before artwork. Specify character references, exact prop
counts and support/contact, geography and continuity with adjacent scenes. Leave
room for language expansion and editable lettering. Check the plan against the
packet and bible, rebuild and validate. Distinguish a completed plan from a
reviewed rendered page; record proof evidence only for actual inspected files.
