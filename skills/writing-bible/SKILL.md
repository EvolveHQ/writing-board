# Maintain the bible

Read [the bible guide](../../docs/bible.md), the current bible, relevant source
records and affected stories. Use the [dossier](../../templates/character-dossier.md)
when developing a character. Add a skeleton with the `character` command if needed;
retain stable IDs when revising an existing character.

Separate sourced facts, established continuity, proposals and unknowns. Cite the
passage or story revision behind each supported assertion. Do not derive canon
from a generated illustration, a draft or a plausible inference. For conflicting
sources, inspect the project's precedence rules and record the resolution or
the unresolved conflict.

Update affected relationships, locations, glossary entries and chronology as
well as the character profile. Keep knowledge changes tied to story beats; a
character cannot act on information learned later. After an accepted story,
re-read for new facts and open promises rather than copying its premise into a
backstory. Validate references, rebuild, and identify stories whose reviews now
need attention. Return a concise change list with evidence and unresolved items.
