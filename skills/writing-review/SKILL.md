# Review and repair

Read the project's required review kinds, current packet and bible. Follow
[the review guide](../../docs/review.md) and use
[the review worksheet](../../templates/review.md) for precise findings. Run
structural validation first, but assess craft independently of its result.

Identify the revision and available artifacts. Trace want, attempts, consequences,
changed choice and payoff. Inspect character knowledge, prop states, relationships
and factual sources against chronology. Check purpose delivery through actions.
For a requested repair, revise within scope and recheck affected beats and pages.

Read-aloud and layout passes must describe what was actually performed. A silent
text pass is not a spoken read-aloud test; an art brief is not a reviewed image.
Record specific findings, reviewer, packet revision and status. Never mark missing
artifacts or unresolved blockers passed. Rebuild after edits, preserve historical
observations and update only review evidence supported by a new inspection.
Return the result, material repairs and remaining work.
